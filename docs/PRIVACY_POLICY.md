## The short version

Freelanche keeps your income records **on your own phone**. We do not have accounts, servers or analytics, so we do not
receive, store, sell or share your income data, your name, or anything else you type into the app.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — your chosen language, your name or nickname, your currency, your monthly income goal, and your
income entries (amounts, dates, notes) — is saved **only on your device**, inside the app's private storage.
It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

* You can export all of it as a CSV file at any time (Settings → Export data).
* You can erase all of it at any time (Settings → Delete all data), or by uninstalling the app.
* If you use Android's built-in backup, your phone may copy the app's data to your own Google account's backup and
  restore it on a new phone. That is controlled by your device settings and handled by Google, not by us.

## Subscriptions and payments

Freelanche is a paid subscription with a free trial, sold through **Google Play**. Google Play handles the payment, the
trial, renewals, cancellation and refunds. **We never see or store your card or bank details.** To know whether your subscription
is active, the app asks Google Play on your phone; that conversation is between your phone and Google, and no information about
you is sent to us. Google's own privacy policy applies to Google Play: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

You can manage or cancel your subscription any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions), or from
Settings → Manage subscription in the app.

## Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. (It opens Android's own file
picker only when you choose to import a CSV file, and only that file is read.)

## Advertising and tracking

There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind.

## Children

Freelanche is not designed for children and is intended for people aged 18 and over.

## Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side; you control all of it
in the app, as described above. If you have a question about this policy, write to [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS].

If the app ever begins to collect or transmit data (for example, if sign-in or cloud backup were added), this policy will be

updated to match.
